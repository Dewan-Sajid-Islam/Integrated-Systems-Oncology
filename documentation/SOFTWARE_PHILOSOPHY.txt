Integrated Systems Oncology Engine
Software Philosophy

1. Biology drives mathematics.
2. Mathematics drives simulation.
3. Simulation drives outputs.
4. Outputs drive figures.
5. Figures support the manuscript.

The manuscript never dictates the simulation.

Every figure must originate from reproducible simulation outputs.

Every equation must have biological meaning.

Every parameter must be explicitly documented.

Every simulation must be reproducible.

The engine is the primary scientific artifact.

The manuscript is one application of the engine.
